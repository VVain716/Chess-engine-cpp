Pawn Structure:
    - Have evaluations that penalize players that have doubled pawns, backward pawns, isolated pawns and multiple different pawn islands.
    - Reward people for passed pawns/protected passed pawns

Rooks:
    - Reward rooks on open files.
    - Reward rooks on the 7th rank
    - Penalize rooks that are trapped in the corner. 

Bishops:
    - Reward Bishops for being on long diagonals and reward for the amount of squares that the bishops can cover
    - Penalize bishops in closed positions
Knights:
    - Reward knights for being centralized and in outposts (places that can never be attacked by pawns)
    - Reward knights for being present in closed positions (not much mobility)
    - Penalize Knights in open positions

Kings:
    - In middlegame, penalize kings for being exposed (not having pawns in front of them)
    - Penalize kings for being on open files.
    - Reward kings for being centralized in the endgame.
